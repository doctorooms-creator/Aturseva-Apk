## Full description — en-IN (~2,300 chars, warm-care, honest)

Aturseva puts the whole visit in your pocket — from finding the right doctor to walking in at the right time.

FIND THE RIGHT DOCTOR
Search by name or problem — fever, knee pain, skin, child care and more. Symptom chips take you to the right specialty in one tap. Every profile shows the fee upfront, clinic hours and registration number — before you commit to anything.

BOOK IN SECONDS — NO SIGN-UP NEEDED
Pick a slot, add your name and phone number, confirm. That is the whole booking. Pay at the clinic itself — no advance payment, no card inside the app, no wallet to top up.

YOUR TOKEN, LIVE
When reception confirms your request, your token appears on its own — the screen updates by itself, you never have to refresh or call the clinic to ask.

TRACK THE QUEUE FROM HOME
See your position in the line while it moves, with an honest estimate of when it is your turn. Leave home at the right time — not two hours early.

EVERY BOOKING ON RECORD
Look up all your appointments with just your phone number — no password to forget, no account needed. Plans changed? Cancel with a reason in one tap. Same doctor again? Book again from your history.

MADE FOR INDIA
Clean English interface, automatic dark mode, light on data. Doctor networks across Indian cities — Delhi, Mumbai, Jaipur, Gandhinagar and more, growing every week.

We do not store prescriptions or medical records in this app. Your visit, your token, your time.

Questions or deletion requests: aturseva.com/contact
